# Content authoring formats

All vocabulary and wiki content is authored as plain UTF-8 text files in this folder and compiled by `scripts/build-content.mjs`
into lazy JSON chunks under `src/data/`. **Never edit generated files by hand.** Lines starting with `#` and blank lines are ignored.
Fields are separated by ` | ` (space-pipe-space). The pipe character must never appear inside a field.
Pinyin, ids and levels are generated by the build – authors only write the fields listed below.

Quality bar for ALL content: accurate, natural, standard terminology, original wording (do not copy dictionary or encyclopedia text),
short example sentences (6–16 words / 8–24 hanzi) that make the meaning of the word clear and contain the word in its base form.

## 1. Chinese topic packs – `content/topics/zh/<pack-id>.txt`

```
@pack | kitchen-tools | Kitchen & Cooking Tools | 厨房与厨具 | 🍳 | home | Cookware, utensils, appliances and culinary essentials.
锅 | pot; pan | 我用大锅煮汤。 | I cook soup in a big pot.
烤箱 | oven | 妈妈用烤箱烤了一个蛋糕。 | Mom baked a cake in the oven.
```
- `theme` ∈ `home nature food health travel work lifestyle social`.
- Word line: `hanzi | english1; english2 | example sentence in hanzi | English translation of the example`.
- EVERY line needs english + example (words already in the HSK library use the library entry; yours is the fallback). Simplified characters.
- Words are keyed by hanzi; each hanzi appears once per file. Include multi-character words and common phrases/collocations, not just single characters.
- Existing packs must keep all words they have today (see `src/data/topicPacks.ts`) – they may be re-listed in your file.
- Size: at least 120 distinct words per pack (broad packs 150–250). Goal: the pack covers ≥80 % of what a learner meets in that area in daily life
  (think: everything a standard textbook chapter, a picture dictionary and a supermarket/hospital/airport/office would use). Mix HSK levels 1–7+ and real-world words beyond the HSK lists.
- Sort easy → hard inside the file.

## 2. English topic lists (for Chinese learners of English) – `content/topics/en/<topic-slug>.txt`

```
@topic | Food & Drink | 🍎
apple | /ˈæpl/ | 苹果 | She eats an apple every morning. | 她每天早上吃一个苹果。 | 1
```
- Word line: `word or phrase | /IPA/ (British or General American, consistent) | 中文释义1; 中文释义2 | example sentence | 例句中文翻译 | level`.
- `level` = CEFR band 1–6 (1=A1 … 6=C2). Spread realistically; roughly 15 % A1, 20 % A2, 25 % B1, 20 % B2, 12 % C1, 8 % C2.
- The Chinese glosses are the key meanings **in this topic** (concise, ≤3 senses). Provide the headword in base form (verbs infinitive without "to", nouns singular).
- A word appears in only ONE topic file overall (the other authors cover other topics – stay inside your topics; if a word clearly belongs to two, choose the more specific topic).
  Multi-word items (phrasal verbs, collocations, idioms) are welcome when common in that topic.
- Size: at least 120 distinct entries per topic (broad topics 180–300). Goal: ≥80 % of the everyday and exam-relevant vocabulary of that area.
- Be careful with the IPA: standard symbols only (ˈ ˌ ː ə ɪ ʊ ʌ æ ɒ ɔ ɑ θ ð ʃ ʒ tʃ dʒ ŋ). Wrong IPA is worse than none – if unsure use a simpler transcription of the standard pronunciation.

## 3. Domain (specialty) courses – `content/domains/<domain-id>.txt`

```
@domain | emotor | Electric Motor Design | 电机设计 | engineering | Terminology of electrical machine design, from machine types to testing.
stator-core | 1 | Machine types and parts | stator core | 定子铁芯 |  | The laminated iron part that carries the magnetic flux around the stator windings. | The stator core is built from thin electrical steel laminations. | 定子铁芯由薄硅钢片叠压而成。 |
igbt | 2 | Power semiconductors | insulated-gate bipolar transistor | 绝缘栅双极型晶体管 | IGBT | A voltage-controlled power switch combining MOSFET gate drive with bipolar conduction. | The IGBT switches the inverter phase current at 10 kHz. | IGBT以10千赫兹切换逆变器相电流。 | eye gee bee tee
```
- Concept line: `concept-id | tier | topic | term_en | term_zh | abbr | definition_en | example_en | example_zh | speak_en`.
  - `concept-id`: unique kebab-case within the file; `tier`: 1 Essentials (core words everybody in the field uses), 2 Professional (standard working vocabulary), 3 Expert (specialist/advanced).
  - `topic`: 8–16 sub-topic names per domain, used for grouping (keep the spelling identical across lines).
  - `abbr`: the standard abbreviation if there is one (empty otherwise, but keep the separators). `speak_en`: only when the English term/abbreviation is not pronounced as written (e.g. "eye gee bee tee"); otherwise empty.
  - `term_zh`: the **standard Chinese technical term** as used in textbooks/standards (GB/T) and industry; `example_zh` must contain `term_zh` verbatim; `example_en` must contain `term_en` (or its plural/inflection).
  - `definition_en`: one precise sentence (≤30 words), original wording.
  - A line has exactly 10 fields (9 separators); empty fields stay empty between separators.
- Size: see assignment (typically 350–500 concepts). Tier balance about 35 % / 40 % / 25 %. Coverage goal: ≥80 % of the terms an practitioner (student → working engineer/clinician/coach) uses day to day.
- Register: engineering = technical prose; medicine = clinical terms plus patient-facing phrases (tag topic "Patient communication"); football = commentary, tactics and slang; Pilates = imperative cueing and anatomy.

## 4. Chinese grammar wiki – `content/wiki/zh/<category>.json`

A JSON array of articles (pretty-printed, UTF-8):
```json
{
  "id": "particle-le",
  "title": "Aspect particle 了",
  "titleHanzi": "了",
  "category": "aspect",
  "hskRange": [1, 4],
  "summary": "One-to-two sentences: what the concept does.",
  "pattern": "Verb + 了 + Object",
  "rules": [
    { "rule": "Plain English explanation, **bold** allowed.", "highlight": "了",
      "examples": [ { "hanzi": "我买了一本书。", "pinyin": "Wǒ mǎi le yì běn shū.", "english": "I bought a book." } ] }
  ],
  "contrasts": [ { "vs": "过", "text": "How the two differ." } ],
  "pitfalls": ["✗ 我昨天去了学校了。 → ✓ 我昨天去学校了。 Why it is wrong."],
  "relatedPointIds": ["g-le", "g2-yao-le"],
  "keywords": ["le", "completed action", "了"],
  "status": "draft"
}
```
- Categories: `sentence-patterns particles aspect complements ba-bei comparison questions negation modal-verbs measure-words connectors time-place formal-written adverbs coverbs`.
- Each article: 3–6 rules, ≥2 examples per rule (6–14 examples per article), 2–5 pitfalls (typical errors of English/German speakers), `relatedPointIds` must exist (valid lesson ids are listed in the assignment), `keywords` contain hanzi, toneless pinyin and English terms.
- Concept hub, not a lesson copy: explain the *why*, compare with neighbours, link to lessons.
- Pinyin: tone marks, syllables separated by spaces per word as in the existing grammar data (`src/data/grammar/hsk1.json`), neutral tone unmarked, tone sandhi NOT applied (write the base tone: 不 → bù even before 4th tone is written as in the dictionary… follow what existing lessons do – check them).
  Verify every example with `pinyin-pro` (installed: `import { pinyin } from 'pinyin-pro'`).

## Build, checks and part files
- `npm run build:content` compiles everything into `src/data/generated` (commit the result); `npm run check:content` validates only (CI regenerates and fails on a diff).
- Long domain files may be split into part files `content/domains/_<domain>-2.txt` … (no header line); the builder appends them to `<domain>.txt`.
- Fields are split at every `|`; a missing last field (`speak_en`) counts as empty.

## 5. English grammar lessons (for Chinese learners) – `src/data/grammarEn/<level>/<slug>.json`

One JSON **object per lesson** (so authors never edit the same file). `<level>` ∈ `a1 a2 b1 b2 c1 c2`; ids start with `e<1-6>-` (a1 = e1 … c2 = e6) and
the file name is the id without that prefix (`e3-passive-simple` → `b1/passive-simple.json`).
Explanations are written in **simplified Chinese** (the course's content language, like the English wiki); every example sentence is **English**.
Original wording; natural, current English; every example carries the target structure.

```json
{
  "id": "e3-passive-simple",
  "cefr": 3,
  "title": "Passive voice (present & past simple) · 被动语态",
  "tag": "be + V3",
  "pattern": "Object + am/is/are/was/were + Past participle + (by Agent)",
  "summary": "一句话概括（中文）。",
  "wikiId": "passive-voice-rules",
  "explanation": ["中文段落，可含 **加粗** 和英文例子。", "…"],
  "mistakes": ["✗ The window broke by him. → ✓ The window was broken by him. 中文说明原因。"],
  "examples": [ { "text": "The room is cleaned every day.", "translation": "房间每天都有人打扫。", "note": "可选中文提示" } ],
  "exercises": [
    { "type": "choice", "prompt": "选择正确的形式。", "sentence": "The letter ___ yesterday.", "translation": "信是昨天寄出的。", "options": ["was sent", "sent", "is send", "was send"], "answer": 0, "explanation": "中文解释。" },
    { "type": "translate", "prompt": "选出正确的英文翻译。", "source": "这座桥建于1990年。", "options": ["The bridge was built in 1990.", "…"], "answer": 0, "explanation": "…" },
    { "type": "error", "prompt": "哪一句是错误的？", "options": ["…", "…", "…"], "answer": 1, "explanation": "…" },
    { "type": "order", "prompt": "把单词排成正确的句子。", "tokens": ["The", "cake", "was", "eaten", "by", "the", "kids."], "alternatives": [], "translation": "蛋糕被孩子们吃掉了。", "explanation": "…" }
  ]
}
```
- `tag`: ≤ 12 characters, shown on the lesson tile (e.g. `be + V3`, `a / an / the`). `pattern`: tokens separated by ` + `, alternatives by two spaces, `·`, two spaces.
- `wikiId` (optional): id of an article in `src/data/englishGrammarWiki.ts` that this lesson practises (valid ids: `five-sentence-patterns attributive-clauses noun-clauses infinitives-to-do gerunds-and-participles conditionals-and-subjunctive modal-verbs-deduction inverted-sentences passive-voice-rules articles-rules`).
- 4–6 `explanation` paragraphs (the *why*, contrast with Chinese habits, when NOT to use it), 2–4 `mistakes` typical for Chinese speakers (each `✗ wrong → ✓ right` + short reason), **5–8 examples** (easy → hard), **8–12 exercises** using at least 3 of the 4 types with the correct answer at varied positions (not always index 0).
- `choice`: exactly one `___`; exactly one option fits; distractors are realistic learner errors. `translate`/`error`: options are full English sentences; the `error` answer is the *wrong* sentence. `order`: 3–12 tokens in the correct order (punctuation attached to the last word, first word capitalised); list other fully correct orders in `alternatives`.
- Check with `node scripts/validate-grammar-en.mjs <level>` (e.g. `b1`) – it must report no problems for your level.

## Rules for authors (subagents)
- Write only your assigned files in `content/`. Do not touch `src/`, `scripts/`, git, or other authors' files.
- Work in several passes (draft in chunks of ~60–100 lines per write, append with further edits) so that no single write is huge.
- Re-read your files at the end: remove duplicates, check separators and field counts, verify sizes. Report counts per file when done (one short line per file).
